# OpalSwift Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy covers personal information that **Miriam Teller, trading as OpalSwift** (not yet registered as a business; free beta), Jerusalem, Israel ("**OpalSwift**", "we") handles **for itself**: about studio owners, admins and staff who use the platform, people who sign up or contact us, and visitors to `opalswift.com`.

It does **not** cover information that studios keep about their own clients (parents, students, photography clients). For that, each studio is responsible and has its own privacy policy; OpalSwift only processes it on the studio's behalf under a [data processing agreement](02-dpa.en.md).

Privacy contact: Miriam Teller, hello@opalswift.com.

## 1. What we collect and why

| Information | Why |
|---|---|
| Name, email, phone, studio name, role | Creating and managing your account, signing you in, contacting you about the service |
| Sign-in data (email one-time codes / magic links, session tokens) | Security and keeping you signed in |
| Billing details (plan, payments, invoices) | Charging fees and keeping accounting records, once fees apply |
| Support messages | Helping you |
| Enquiries through the contact form on `opalswift.com`: your name, email and message | Answering you and following up on your enquiry, because you asked us to contact you. We email each enquiry to ourselves and keep it in our own customer list (OpalSwift's account on the platform) |
| Technical data (IP address, browser, time of request, error messages) | Running and securing the service, preventing abuse, fixing faults. Kept in hosting-provider logs for a short period |
| Records of actions in your studio account (audit log) | Security and accountability |
| Marketing preference | Product news **only if you opt in**. Service and security messages are sent regardless |

You are not legally required to give us this information, but we cannot open an account without your name and email.

We do **not** use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell personal information.

## 2. Who we share it with

Only service providers who help us run the platform: Supabase (database and sign-in; EU, Frankfurt), Cloudflare (hosting, email, encrypted backups), Resend (sending email, including contact-form enquiries), GitHub (backup job and code), and if you use them, Twilio (SMS/WhatsApp) and Google (calendar); and Sentry (error monitoring, EU region, configured to exclude IP addresses and personal details). A problem report sent to Sentry includes a screenshot only if a studio admin chooses to attach one; the admin can black out details first, or send a snapshot with all text and images hidden. A screenshot may still show personal data visible on that screen. Some are US companies and may access data from outside Israel; we rely on transfers allowed by Israeli regulations (EU adequacy / Convention 108 countries, and the providers' written undertakings). We also disclose information if the law requires it.

## 3. How long we keep it

- Account data: while the account is open and up to 24 months after, unless you ask us to delete it sooner and we do not need it for a legal reason.
- Website enquiries: up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
- Billing records: as long as tax law requires (7 years).
- Technical logs: per the hosting provider's retention (short-term); our own audit log for 24 months.
- Backups: nightly backups 30 days, monthly backups about 13 months.

## 4. Your rights

You may ask to see (§13 Privacy Protection Law) and correct or delete (§14) your information, and withdraw marketing consent at any time. Write to hello@opalswift.com. We answer within 21 days. You may also complain to the Privacy Protection Authority.

## 5. Security

See our [Information Security Procedure summary](11-security-procedure.en.md): tenant data isolation in the database, encryption in transit, encrypted secrets, nightly encrypted backups, least-privilege access, and an incident response plan.

## 6. Changes

We will post updates here and email account owners about significant changes.
