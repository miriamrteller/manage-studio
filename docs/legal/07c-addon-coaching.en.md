# Add-on: Coaching and tutoring sessions

## A. Sessions and packages

- A session lasts [[NUMBER]] minutes, [[in person at [[PLACE]] / online by video]].
- A package of [[NUMBER]] sessions is valid for **[[NUMBER]] months** from the first session. Unused sessions after that expire [[/ may be extended once by [[NUMBER]] months on request]]; sessions are not transferable to another person [[unless agreed]].
- Price per session or package: as in the agreement. A package is paid [[in full in advance / in N instalments]].

## B. Booking, lateness and no-shows

- Sessions are booked [[in the app / by message]] and confirmed by the Provider.
- A session cancelled by the Client **at least [[24]] hours** before its start is rebooked at no charge. A session cancelled later, or missed without notice, **counts as used** [[/ is charged at [[50]]%]], except in an emergency, at the Provider's discretion.
- If the Client arrives late, the session still ends at the planned time. If the Provider is late, the time is made up or the session is extended.
- A session cancelled by the Provider is rebooked at no charge or, if the Client prefers, refunded.

## C. What the Client can expect

- The Provider prepares and delivers the sessions with professional care, and gives honest feedback on progress. Progress depends on the Client's own work and circumstances; the Provider does not guarantee a result such as a grade, a placing or a fitness goal.
- Materials the Provider gives (worksheets, plans, recordings) are for the Client's personal use and stay the Provider's property.
- Online sessions are not recorded by either side [[unless both agree]].

## D. Minors

For a Client under 18, the parent or legal guardian signs, is the contact for bookings and payments, and [[is present / may be present]] during sessions. For physical training the participant also completes a [health declaration](07b-health-declaration-child.en.md) ([adult version](07b-health-declaration-adult.en.md)).

### Drafting notes

- **App wiring.** The app attaches this add-on when the studio's field is set to it on the platform's Skins page, or, if the field sets nothing, when the field's name says tutor, coach or therapist. The agreement is then called "Sessions agreement". Until the studio edits its form, the blanks are filled with: 60-minute sessions, in person or online by video; a package of 10 sessions valid for 6 months, which can be extended once by 3 months on request; paid in full in advance; booked in the app or by message; a late cancellation counts as used (no extra charge); a parent may be present. [[LAWYER: confirm]]
- **Expiry of prepaid sessions.** An expiry that leaves the Client with nothing for a prepaid package may be read as unfair in a standard contract (Standard Contracts Law §4); the extension option in section A and a reasonable validity period reduce that risk — **verify with lawyer**.
- **Language.** If the English and Hebrew versions differ, the English version prevails.
