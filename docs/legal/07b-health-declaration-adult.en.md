# Health Declaration — Adult participant (18+)

**Participant:** [[full name]] · Date of birth: [[ ]] · Activity / group: [[ ]]

## 1. Questions

Tick yes or no. If "yes", add a short note.

1. Has a doctor ever said you have a heart condition, or that you should do physical activity only under medical supervision?
2. In the last 6 months, have you had chest pain, fainting, or loss of balance from dizziness during or after exercise?
3. Do you have asthma, epilepsy, diabetes or another condition that may need a response during the activity?
4. Have you had an injury, fracture or surgery in the last 6 months, or do you have a joint, back or bone problem?
5. Do you have an allergy (food, medication, insect stings) that the staff should know about? Do you carry an EpiPen or inhaler?
6. Do you take regular medication, or has a doctor advised you that a current health condition limits physical activity?
7. Are you pregnant, or is there anything else that would help the instructor support you? *(optional)*
8. For water activities only: can you swim unaided? [[STUDIO: remove this question if the activity is not in water]]

## 2. Declaration

- To the best of my knowledge the answers are correct and complete.
- If I answered "yes" to questions 1–4, I will consult my doctor, and the studio may ask for a doctor's note before I take part.
- I will tell the studio promptly about any change in my health.

## 3. Emergency

- Emergency contact: [[name, relation, phone]].
- In an emergency, I authorise the studio staff to call emergency services (מד"א 101) and to give basic first aid until help arrives.

### Drafting notes (not shown to participants)

- Same legal position as the child version: good practice for ordinary classes; mandatory under the Gyms Law for a licensed gym (use the prescribed form — **verify**); the Sports Law applies to competitive athletes and recognised training institutions (**verify** if participants compete); pools have their own rules (**verify**).
- Health information is information of special sensitivity (Privacy Protection Law as amended): ask only what is needed, restrict access, delete after the retention period.
- Keep it a declaration only: no release of liability (Standard Contracts Law §4(1)). Photo consent is separate ([09-in-app-consent-texts](09-in-app-consent-texts.md)).
- **Language.** If the English and Hebrew versions differ, the English version prevails.
