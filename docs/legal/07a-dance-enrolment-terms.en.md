## 3. Missed classes and make-up lessons

- A class the **studio** cancels will be made up on another date or credited/refunded proportionally.
- A class the **student** misses (illness, holiday, etc.) is not refunded. You may book a make-up lesson in a suitable class within [[30 days]], subject to space, up to [[MAX MAKE-UPS]] make-ups per season.
- If the studio cannot operate for a long period for reasons beyond its control (for example a security situation and Home Front Command instructions), we will offer online lessons, make-up lessons or a proportional credit/refund. ([[LAWYER: check current guidance for emergency periods]])

## 4. Cancelling the enrolment

You can cancel by email to [[EMAIL]], by phone [[PHONE]], via the parent portal [[if available]], or in person. We will confirm in writing.

**a. Within 14 days of enrolling online or by phone (distance sale):**
You may cancel within 14 days from the day of enrolment or the day you received these terms, whichever is later. If classes have not started, you get a full refund less a cancellation fee of **5% of the price or ₪100, whichever is lower**. If classes have started, you also pay for classes already given. [[LAWYER: check whether the "leisure services starting within 7 days" exception in §14C(d) applies to classes; if it does, state it here.]]
People aged 65+, people with disabilities and new immigrants may have up to 4 months to cancel in some cases (§14C1). 

**b. At any time during the season (continuing service):**
Because the season is an ongoing service, you may end it at any time. The enrolment ends **no later than 3 business days** after we receive your notice. You pay only for the period until then; any amount paid for later classes is refunded within [[14]] days. [[If a season discount was given for committing to the full season: LAWYER to confirm whether and how it may be adjusted.]]

**c. If the studio cancels a class group** (for example too few students), you get a full refund for the classes not given.

Refunds are paid in the way you paid, or as agreed.

## 5. Health and safety

- Before the first class, the parent fills in the **health declaration** in the app and updates it if anything changes.
- Please tell the teacher about any injury or condition affecting participation. The teacher may ask a student to sit out an activity for safety.
- Emergency contact: the parent gives at least one emergency contact who can be reached during class.
- For students under [[AGE]]: a parent or an adult they authorise must bring and collect the student at the door of the studio. Tell us in writing who may collect the student.
- The studio holds [[third-party liability insurance — insurer / policy]] ([[STUDIO: confirm]]).

## 6. Conduct

We expect respectful behaviour toward teachers, students and parents. Dress code: [[DRESS CODE]]. Watching classes: [[e.g. only at open lessons]]. Filming classes by parents: [[e.g. not allowed, to protect other children's privacy]]. If a student's behaviour repeatedly puts others at risk, after talking with the parent we may end the enrolment with a proportional refund.

## 7. Photos and video

Taking part in classes **does not depend** on agreeing to photos. Consent to photographing the student and to using photos/video is asked **separately** in the enrolment form, by purpose (internal use, parent groups, website/social media). You may change your choice at any time in the portal or by email.

## 8. Privacy

How we handle personal information, including health information and information about children, is explained in our [Privacy Policy](03-studio-privacy-policy.en.md).

## 9. Messages

We send service messages about classes, payments and changes by email, SMS or WhatsApp. Marketing messages are sent only if you opt in, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

## 10. General

Israeli law applies. If these terms and the law differ, the law that protects the consumer prevails. Questions: [[EMAIL / PHONE]].

### Sources relied on
- Consumer Protection Law 5741-1981: §13C–13D (continuing transaction — consumer may cancel at any time; cancellation effective within 3 business days; pay only for the period until then); §14C (distance sale — 14 days to cancel, required disclosure, cancellation fee cap, refund); §14C(d) (exceptions — *verify whether classes/leisure exception applies*); §14C1 (extended period for seniors, people with disabilities, new immigrants — *verify conditions*).
- Consumer Protection (Cancellation of Transaction) Regulations 5771-2010: cancellation fee — 5% of the price or ₪100, the lower; refund rules. *Secondary sources mention a specific rule for courses/classes in reg. 2 — verify with lawyer.*
- Standard Contracts Law 5743-1982, §4 (unfair terms — avoid one-sided cancellation and liability terms).
- Privacy Protection Law 5741-1981, §2(4)/§2(6) (image use requires consent).
- No case law is cited.
