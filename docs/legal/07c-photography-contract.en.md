## 2. Price, deposit and balance

- Total price: {{price}} [[incl. VAT / no VAT — עוסק פטור]].
- **Deposit:** [[%]] of the price, paid when signing, to reserve the date.
- **Balance:** paid [[before the shoot / before gallery delivery / in N instalments]] through the payment link the Photographer sends (YPay or another provider).
- The Photographer issues a tax document (receipt / tax invoice) for each payment.

## 3. Cancellation and rescheduling

**a. Cancellation within 14 days of signing (if the agreement was made online or by phone):**
The Client may cancel within 14 days of signing (or of receiving this agreement, if later) and gets a refund less a cancellation fee of **5% of the price or ₪100, whichever is lower**. [[LAWYER: check whether the §14C(d) exception for leisure/event services due to start within 7 days applies to a photography service, and state the result here.]]

**b. Cancellation by the Client after that:**
- More than [[60]] days before the shoot: [[deposit refunded less ₪X / deposit kept]].
- [[60–14]] days before: [[the deposit is kept]].
- Less than [[14]] days before: [[deposit kept and X% of the balance is due]].
These amounts are agreed compensation that reflects the Photographer's reasonable loss from a reserved date. ([[LAWYER: agreed compensation must bear a reasonable relation to the expected damage — Contracts (Remedies) Law §15. Check the amounts.]])

**c. Rescheduling:** one change of date is free if requested at least [[14]] days before and a new date is available within [[6 months]]. Otherwise it is treated as cancellation.

**d. Weather (outdoor shoots):** if weather makes the shoot impossible, both sides agree a new date at no charge.

**e. Cancellation by the Photographer:** if the Photographer cannot attend for a reason within their control, the Client gets a full refund. If it is due to illness or emergency, the Photographer will try to send a professional substitute of similar level, with the Client's agreement; otherwise a full refund.

## 4. Delivery

- Edited images are delivered within **[[NUMBER]] days** of the shoot (or of full payment, if later) through a private online gallery at [[GALLERY SERVICE]].
- The gallery stays online for [[NUMBER]] months. Please download your images; after that the Photographer keeps an archive copy for [[NUMBER]] months only.
- Selection and editing style are at the Photographer's professional discretion, in line with the portfolio the Client has seen. Raw (unedited) files are not delivered [[unless agreed]].

## 5. Copyright and use of the images

- **The Photographer owns the copyright in all images.** The parties expressly agree to this. *(Without this clause, Israeli law gives copyright in a commissioned portrait or private/family event photo to the person who ordered it — Copyright Law §35(b).)*
- The Client receives a **non-exclusive, perpetual licence for personal use**: printing, sharing with family and friends, and posting on personal social-media accounts. Commercial use, sale, or entering images in competitions requires the Photographer's written consent.
- Please credit the Photographer ([[@HANDLE]]) when posting where practical, and do not apply filters that substantially change an image while crediting it — the Photographer's moral rights (§§45–46).

## 6. Portfolio and marketing (separate, optional consent)

The Photographer will use images for portfolio, website, social media or advertising **only if the Client ticks the separate consent box** below the agreement. The Client may withdraw it at any time for future use. Refusing does not affect the service or price.

## 7. Minors

If minors are photographed, the Client confirms they are their parent/legal guardian or have the guardian's consent. Images of minors are **never** used for portfolio or marketing without the guardian's explicit, separate consent.

## 8. Client's responsibilities

The Client arranges any permits for the location, tells guests that a photographer is present, and makes sure the Photographer can work safely. Delays caused by the Client may shorten the shooting time.

## 9. Liability

- The Photographer uses professional equipment and backs up images [[on two cards / immediately after the shoot]].
- If images are lost or damaged because of equipment failure, theft or another reason beyond the Photographer's reasonable control, the Photographer's liability is limited to **refunding the amounts paid** for the affected part of the service [[or a free re-shoot, where possible]].
- This limit does not apply to damage caused intentionally or by gross negligence, or where the law does not allow a limit.

## 10. Force majeure

Neither party is responsible for a delay or failure caused by events beyond its control (for example war, a security situation, Home Front Command restrictions, natural disaster, epidemic). The parties will agree a new date; if that is not possible within [[6 months]], amounts paid are refunded less reasonable costs already incurred ([[LAWYER: confirm]]).

## 11. Privacy

Personal information is handled under the Photographer's [Privacy Policy](03-studio-privacy-policy.en.md).

### Sources relied on
- Copyright Law 5768-2007: §33 (author is first owner); **§35(b)** (in a portrait or private/family event photo commissioned for private purposes, the commissioner is the first owner unless agreed otherwise); §37 (transfer / exclusive licence must be in writing); §§45–46 (moral rights: attribution and integrity).
- Consumer Protection Law 5741-1981: §14C (distance sale: 14 days, disclosure, fee cap); §14C(d) (exceptions — *verify for photography/events*).
- Consumer Protection (Cancellation of Transaction) Regulations 5771-2010 (fee: 5% or ₪100, the lower).
- Contracts (Remedies for Breach of Contract) Law 5731-1970, §15 (agreed compensation; the court may reduce it if it bears no reasonable relation to foreseeable damage).
- Standard Contracts Law 5743-1982, §4.
- Privacy Protection Law 5741-1981, §2(6) (use of a person's image for profit without consent is an infringement of privacy).
- Electronic Signature Law 5761-2001 (see doc 09 for the level of the typed-name signature).
