## 2. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only as needed:

- **OpalSwift** (our software provider) and its service providers for hosting, storage, email and backups (Supabase — servers in Frankfurt, Germany; Cloudflare; and, if used, Twilio for SMS/WhatsApp and Google Calendar);
- **payment and invoicing providers** we use: [[LIST — e.g. YPay, Grow, iCount]];
- [[GALLERY SERVICE]] (photography);
- our staff and teachers, only as they need for their work;
- authorities, when the law requires it (for example the tax authority).

Some of these providers store or access data outside Israel (in the EU and the USA). We transfer data abroad only as Israeli law allows, mainly to the EU, which provides adequate protection, or under the provider's written undertaking to protect it.

## 3. How long we keep it

- Account, class and booking records: for as long as you are a client and 7 years after (the limitation period for legal claims).
- Payment and tax records: as long as tax law requires ([[ACCOUNTANT: usually 7 years]]).
- Health declarations: the health details are deleted [[e.g. 1]] year after the season; the signed record is kept with signed documents (below), with access restricted.
- Signed waivers/contracts: 7 years after the end of the service; for a child, until the child turns 25 (limitation periods do not run while a claimant is a minor).
- Marketing consent records: as long as we send you marketing, plus [[e.g. 3]] years as proof of consent.

## 4. Your rights

- **See** the information we hold about you (and your child) — Privacy Protection Law §13.
- **Correct or delete** information that is wrong, incomplete, unclear or out of date — §14.
- **Withdraw consent** to photos/media or marketing at any time. Withdrawal does not affect use before it.
- **Stop marketing**: use the unsubscribe link or reply "הסר" / "STOP".
- Ask us to delete information we no longer need. We may keep what the law requires (for example tax records) or what is needed for a legal claim.

Send requests to [[EMAIL]]. We will answer within **21 days**. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the **Privacy Protection Authority** (הרשות להגנת הפרטיות), Ministry of Justice.

## 5. Security

Access to information is restricted to people who need it; our platform separates each studio's data, encrypts connections and keeps backups. No system is perfectly secure; if a serious incident affects your information we will act as the law requires, including telling you where needed.

## 6. Cookies and local storage

Our booking site uses only storage needed for it to work (sign-in, language, unfinished forms). There are no advertising or analytics trackers. See [Cookie & Storage Notice](05-cookie-storage-notice.en.md).

## 7. Changes

We will publish any update here and, if it is significant, tell you by email.
