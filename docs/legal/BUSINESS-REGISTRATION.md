# Business registration for OpalSwift: עוסק פטור, עוסק מורשה or a company

> **General information, not tax or legal advice.** Figures are for 2026 and change every January. **Verify with an accountant** before acting; the steps marked ⚙ need one.

## Short answer

OpalSwift can most likely start as an **עוסק פטור** (VAT-exempt dealer): software and SaaS are not on the list of occupations that must register as עוסק מורשה, and expected turnover is far below the ceiling. Register **before the first paid plan**, not for the free beta. Move to עוסק מורשה when turnover approaches the ceiling, or earlier if business customers want tax invoices.

## עוסק פטור in one table

| Topic | What it means | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Turnover ceiling** | **₪122,833** a year for 2026 (₪120,000 in 2024–25). Counts gross turnover, not profit. Indexed every year. | Tax Authority FAQ on the small-business reform; VAT Registration Regulations 5736-1976 |
| **Excluded occupations** | Regulation 13 lists occupations that must be עוסק מורשה whatever the turnover: lawyers, accountants, doctors and health professions, architects and engineers, management and organisation consultants, insurance agents, driving-school owners, **owners of a school or kindergarten that is not a non-profit**, companies, and others. Software is not listed. | Reg. 13, VAT Registration Regulations. **Verify** against the current text; the list is old and the secondary summaries differ. |
| **VAT** | You do not charge VAT (18%) and cannot reclaim VAT you pay. Example: a provider plan of ₪500 + VAT costs you ₪590; the ₪90 is simply a cost. An עוסק מורשה charges 18% to customers and reclaims the ₪90. | VAT Law 5736-1975 §31(3) |
| **Documents you issue** | A **receipt (קבלה)** for every payment; an invoice is a **חשבונית עסקה**, never a חשבונית מס. Numbered, kept 7 years. | Income Tax (Bookkeeping) Regulations; Tax Authority guidance |
| **Yearly VAT duty** | Declare the past year's turnover by **31 January** on the Tax Authority's עוסק פטור portal. No activity in the year: ask to close the file. | Reg. 15, VAT Registration Regulations; misim.gov.il/empatur |
| **Bookkeeping** | An income-and-expenses ledger is usually enough (single entry). | Income Tax (Bookkeeping) Regulations 5733-1973 |
| **Income tax** | Open an income-tax file; pay advances if assessed; file an annual return (form 1301). Profit is taxed at the personal brackets, after credit points. | Income Tax Ordinance |
| **National Insurance** | Register as עצמאי. 2026 rates on monthly income: **7.7%** up to ₪7,703 (4.47% NI + 3.23% health), **18%** from there to ₪51,910 (12.83% + 5.17%). Someone counted as self-employed pays at least on a minimum income of **₪3,442** a month (minimum advance ₪265). Working fewer than 20 hours a week with low income may count as "not self-employed", with different rules. | btl.gov.il, self-employed rates and 2026 advances circular |

## Two things to watch

1. **Creative Ballet.** Regulation 13's "owner of a school or kindergarten" line may or may not reach a private dance school. ⚙ Ask the accountant before choosing the school's status: if it applies, the school must be עוסק מורשה from day one, whatever its turnover.
2. **Owner with a salary too.** Someone who is also an employee pays National Insurance differently on the business income, and may be below the self-employed definition. ⚙ Accountant.

## When to change

| Move to | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **עוסק מורשה** | Turnover is about to cross the ceiling (tell the Tax Authority before it happens), or business customers want tax invoices, or VAT on your costs is significant | Must at the ceiling; optional before. Monthly or bi-monthly VAT returns start. |
| **Company (בע"מ)** | Profits are high enough that corporate tax planning helps, there are investors or partners, or the liability shield matters | Costs: incorporation, an accountant, annual fees and audited reports. A company can never be עוסק פטור. |

## Steps, and who does them

1. ⚙ Choose the status (פטור vs מורשה; sole trader vs company) with an accountant.
2. Open the VAT file online (free): gov.il "request to open an exempt-dealer file via the internet".
3. Open the income-tax file and register with National Insurance as self-employed (the accountant usually does both).
4. Set up receipts: the app's invoicing providers (Green Invoice, iCount, Invoice4U) issue קבלה / חשבונית עסקה for a פטור.
5. ⚙ Advances and the annual return: accountant.
6. Every January: the turnover declaration (פטור) and a check against the new ceiling.
