# Add-on: Photography

## A. Delivery

- Edited images are delivered within **[[NUMBER]] days** of the shoot (or of full payment, if later) through a private online gallery at [[GALLERY SERVICE]] [[/ as files]].
- The gallery stays online for [[NUMBER]] months. Please download your images; after that the Photographer keeps an archive copy for [[NUMBER]] months only.
- Selection and editing style are at the Photographer's professional discretion, in line with the portfolio the Client has seen. Raw (unedited) files are not delivered [[unless agreed]].
- Weather (outdoor shoots): if weather makes the shoot impossible, both sides agree a new date at no charge.

## B. Copyright and use of the images

- **The Photographer owns the copyright in all images.** The parties expressly agree to this. *(Without this clause, Israeli law gives copyright in a commissioned portrait or private/family event photo to the person who ordered it — Copyright Law §35(b).)*
- The Client receives a **non-exclusive, perpetual licence for personal use**: printing, sharing with family and friends, and posting on personal social-media accounts. Commercial use, sale, or entering images in competitions requires the Photographer's written consent.
- Please credit the Photographer ([[@HANDLE]]) when posting where practical, and do not apply filters that substantially change an image while crediting it — the Photographer's moral rights (§§45–46).

## C. People photographed

- The Client confirms that the people to be photographed know a photographer is present and, where needed, have agreed to be photographed. The Client arranges any permits the location requires and makes sure the Photographer can work safely.
- If minors are photographed, the Client confirms they are their parent or legal guardian, or have the guardian's consent. Images of minors are **never** used for portfolio or marketing without the guardian's explicit, separate consent.

## D. Portfolio and marketing (separate, optional consent)

The Photographer will use images for portfolio, website, social media or advertising **only if the Client ticks the separate consent box** below the agreement. The Client may withdraw it at any time for future use. Refusing does not affect the service or price.

## E. Loss of images

The Photographer uses professional equipment and backs up images [[on two cards / immediately after the shoot]]. If images are lost or damaged for a reason beyond the Photographer's reasonable control, section 6 of the agreement applies (refund of the amounts paid for the affected part [[or a free re-shoot, where possible]]).

### Drafting notes

- **Keep clause B.** Without it the client owns the copyright in private commissions (Copyright Law §35(b)). A typed-name signature is enough here because the clause is an agreement on first ownership, not a transfer under §37 (see OPEN-QUESTIONS R9).
- **Language.** If the English and Hebrew versions differ, the English version prevails.

### Sources relied on
- Copyright Law 5768-2007: §33 (author is first owner); **§35(b)** (commissioned portrait or private/family event photo: the commissioner is first owner unless agreed otherwise); §37 (transfer or exclusive licence in writing); §§45–46 (moral rights).
- Privacy Protection Law 5741-1981, §2(4) and §2(6) (photographing a person in private, and using a person's image for profit, without consent).
