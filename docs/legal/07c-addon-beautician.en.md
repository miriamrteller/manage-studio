# Add-on: Beauty and treatment services

## A. Health and allergy questions

Before the first treatment, and whenever something changes, the Client answers these questions. Tick yes or no; if "yes", add a short note.

1. Do you have a skin condition, an open wound, an infection or inflammation in the treatment area?
2. Do you have a known allergy or sensitivity (cosmetics, hair dye, latex, nickel, essential oils, medication)?
3. Are you pregnant or breastfeeding?
4. Do you take medication that affects the skin or healing (for example retinoids, blood thinners, steroids), or have you had a medical or cosmetic procedure in the treatment area in the last [[3]] months?
5. Do you have a condition the Provider should know about (diabetes, epilepsy, a heart condition, a pacemaker, a tendency to scar)?

The Client confirms the answers are correct to the best of their knowledge and will tell the Provider about any change.

## B. Patch test

For treatments that involve dyes, adhesives, chemical products or heat on the skin ([[list: hair colour, lash extensions, peels, waxing, and so on]]) the Provider carries out a patch test at least **[[48]] hours** before the first treatment. A treatment without a required patch test is at the Client's request and risk, and the Provider may decline it.

## C. Right to decline or stop a treatment

The Provider may decline, change or stop a treatment when it is not safe or suitable: for example a contra-indication in section A, signs of infection or irritation, a reaction during the treatment, or a result that cannot be achieved as asked. A treatment stopped for a safety reason is charged only for the part already given.

## D. Results and aftercare

- Results vary from person to person. The Provider describes the expected result honestly and does not guarantee a specific outcome.
- The Provider gives aftercare instructions ([[orally / in writing / in the app]]). The Client follows them; a reaction or a poorer result caused by not following them is not the Provider's responsibility.
- If a reaction appears, the Client tells the Provider as soon as possible and seeks medical help when needed.

## E. Minors

A treatment for a person under 18 requires the parent's or legal guardian's written consent and, for [[treatments that require it]], the guardian's presence.

## F. Photos of results

Photos before and after a treatment are taken and used for the Provider's portfolio or marketing **only with the Client's separate consent**, which can be withdrawn at any time for future use. Photos kept for the Client's file are personal information under the Provider's Privacy Policy.
